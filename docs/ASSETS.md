# How every asset is made

All assets are generated by code in `deadhouse/assetgen/`. Nothing external is used: no asset packs, no
stock art, no samples, no fonts, no AI services. `python tools/build_assets.py` regenerates everything
into `assets/generated/` and writes `manifest.json` with SHA-256 hashes. A test checks that art generation
reproduces the committed hashes byte for byte. Every generator takes explicit seeds.

| Category | Source | Method |
|---|---|---|
| **Font** ("Corvane Condensed") | `assetgen/font_data.py` | 89 hand-designed glyphs typed as `#`/`.` grids (caps 5×8, proportional, descenders). Used for the UI and for every in-world sign. |
| **Palette** | `assetgen/palette.py` | Hand-picked albedo materials (deliberately mid-valued) and emissive neon/bulb colours. Lighting supplies the mood. |
| **Environments** | `assetgen/env.py`, `bake_row.py`, `bake_tenement.py`, `bake_alley.py`, `bake_marquee.py` | Procedural drawing with pixel primitives: brick and tile walls, sash windows (dark/lit/curtained/blinds), storefronts, doors, cornices, awnings, neon tube letters (glyph outlines), bulb signs, wet street with rails and puddles, skyline, the El, interiors (wallpaper, planks, stairs, furniture). Each bake writes albedo, emissive and material layers plus `meta.json` (colliders, lights, props, anchors). |
| **Characters** | `assetgen/chars.py`, `char_specs.py` | A procedural pixel **rig**. Hand-drawn 9×9 head and hair templates per view (S/N/E) sit on a parametric body (legs, torso/coat, arms as pixel capsules with 2-bone IK), dressed by an outfit spec and posed by per-animation pose functions (idle, walk, run, 3 attacks, heavy, dodge, hit, stagger, down, throw, aim/shoot, climb, interact, takedown, break, talk, smoke, windup, blinded, match). A shading pass gives form and an outline pass gives the ink line. Hand anchors are exported per frame so weapons, the lantern and umbrellas attach at runtime. 16 characters. |
| **Portraits** | `assetgen/portraits.py` | "Ink and light" vector illustration. Hand-placed Bézier shapes are rendered at 3× and downsampled. Lighting is painted as hard-edged planes: warm key light, magenta neon rim, violet shadows. Expressions are parameters (brows, lids, lower lids, mouth corners, parting, gaze, head tilt). Vivienne has 4 expressions. |
| **Icons** | `assetgen/icons.py` | 13 hand-placed 14×14 pixel icons. |
| **Film on the cinema screen** | `bake_marquee.py` | Six "1931 film" frames drawn procedurally with film grain. |
| **App icon** | `tools/build_exe.py` | A neon "D" from the game font, packed into a hand-written .ico container. |
| **Music** | `assetgen/audio/music.py` | *"Two A.M. at the Velour"*: a composed 32-bar AABA ballad in D minor at 84 bpm, swung. The score is in code: chords, voicings, two melodies, an algorithmic walking bass. Synthesised with Karplus-Strong upright bass, an additive inharmonic piano, an FM vibraphone with motor tremolo, an additive clarinet, and noise-shaped brushes, ride and kick. It is mixed with convolution reverb from a synthesised impulse response, and the loop tail is wrapped for a seamless loop. Three renders come from one performance: **inside**, **through the wall** (low-passed, long street reverb) and **apartment radio** (band-passed, saturated, crackle). |
| **Ambience** | `assetgen/audio/sfx.py` | Seamless loops: street rain (pink noise, filtered drop impulses, gutter trickle, metal pings), distant city (brown-noise rumble, far horns, a siren), tenement interior (mains hum, pipes, drips, rain on the roof), cinema (projector whir and 24 fps shutter flutter), alley, rooftop wind, combat **tension** (bowed low strings a minor second apart with a heartbeat pulse). |
| **Sound effects** | `assetgen/audio/sfx.py` | 57 files (about 40 distinct sounds plus variants) from oscillators and filtered noise: footsteps on wet stone, wood and concrete; whooshes; punches; cuts; bullet hits; gunshots with city slap-back echoes; ricochet; glass; bulb pop; match; lantern; flash; body fall; takedown; wood strain and break; door; El train pass (rumble, wheel clacks, screech, panned); UI ticks and plucks; typewriter; the Remnant whisper; alert sting; going down; dressing. |
| **Writing** | `content/dialogue/*.dlg`, `content/areas/*.py`, `content/items.py`, `content/leads.py` | Written for this project: 89 dialogue nodes (about 400 script lines), plus barks, vignettes, item text, leads and hints. |

## Tools for checking assets without eyes or ears
* `tools/preview.py`: crop and upscale any image for inspection.
* `tools/audio_report.py`: peak, RMS, clipping and stereo correlation, plus a log-frequency spectrogram.
  It was used to check that the mix is balanced and the melodies are present.
* `tools/capture.py`: scripted screenshots from the real renderer.
* `tools/render_samples.py`: offline scene mixes for listening (`deliverables/audio/`).
