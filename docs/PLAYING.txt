DEADHOUSE - Prototype 1: "One Block of Lantern Row"
====================================================

Run Deadhouse.exe. F11 toggles fullscreen (or launch with --fullscreen). Esc pauses and shows controls.

KEYBOARD
  WASD / arrows ... move            Shift ... sprint (noisy)
  J ... attack (behind an unaware enemy: takedown)     K ... heavy attack
  Space ... dodge                   E ... interact / talk / climb / pick up
  Q ... throw bottle (aims at lamps and bulbs)          L ... fire revolver (LOUD)
  F ... lantern on/off              C ... lantern flash (blinds)
  H ... field dressing (heal)       R ... ask your Remnant for a hint
  Tab ... inventory, evidence, leads                     Esc ... pause / controls
  Xbox-style pads are mapped too (untested).

WHAT IS HERE (30-45 minutes)
  Lantern Row on Night Two. Talk to people. Augie the photographer wants his camera back from Hobb,
  who keeps the top floor of No. 14 (the shabby tenement at the west end of the block).

  No. 14 can be taken several ways. Try more than one:
   * Head-on: fight up the stairs. Dodge through wind-ups. The gunman at the top aims with a red line.
   * Darkness: throw a bottle at the hall bulb from the doorway, let them come to look, take them from
     behind.
   * Outside: the alley beside No. 14 has a fire escape (Silas lets you climb). It reaches the roofs and
     Hobb's window. Hobb watches his door, not his window.
   * Lantern: the lantern is somewhere on the roofs. Its flash blinds anyone looking at you.
   * Firing the revolver is loud. In No. 14 it brings more men in from the street.

  Also: the Velour (Vivienne Sabler outside, Bruno at the door), Sully under the dead cinema, Rosa in the
  alley, the rooftops, and the Marquee. Its boarded staff door needs Maud Kessel's Lesson. In this
  prototype you get her from the PROTOTYPE TEST KIT in the morgue wagon. In the real game she is earned
  in the Sumps.

  How you speak to people ([cold] [warm] [wry] [blunt]) and what you do are remembered.

All art, animation, music, sound, fonts and writing in this game were generated by code written for this
project. No external assets.
