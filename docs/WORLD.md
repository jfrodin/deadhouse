# World and narrative

## Corvane
An industrial port city on a river delta, in a mid-century that never existed. It is always night when we
are there.

| District | Identity | Light | Status |

## The central mystery (proposed arc)
Bodies are arriving at the Deadhouse **hollow**: no last hour to take. Someone is extracting the final
hours of the dying, sometimes of people who were made to die, and distilling them into **Last Call**,
which the Heights consume in private rooms. The supply chain runs through the Sumps (Dravo's cellar,
"donors"), the Row (the Velour's private rooms, Lionel Gaunt) and up to the Heights. Its darkest product,
the last hours of victims of violence, is the story's moral low point. It is never depicted; the horror is
that someone buys it.

## Prototype 1 content: Night Two, Lantern Row (implemented)

**Places**
* The street, about three screens wide:
  * No. 14, the Garnet Arms (a tenement with a late laundromat)
  * the Hotel Calla (rooms by the hour)
  * the Velour (club; a black tile and gold deco facade)
  * Prell & Son Loans
  * the Marquee (a cinema closed for renovation in 1931)
  * Orsolya's diner, closed for a funeral
  * a garage
  * the Elevated Line overhead
* **No. 14 interior:** four floors, the Dunne brothers, Pike, and Hobb's room.
* **The alley beside No. 14:** laundry lines, a hotel kitchen door, the fire escape.
* **The roofs:** the pigeon loft, a water tower, the back of the VELOUR sign, the city beyond.
* **Inside the Marquee:** a projector still running a 1931 promotional film for Saint Agathe's Hospital.

**People** (all adults)
* **Ines Calder:** protagonist; an ex-nurse from Saint Agathe's.
* **Silas Webb:** her first Remnant, a sardonic dead cat burglar.
* **Maud Kessel:** a dead bare-knuckle fighter (prototype grant only).
* **Vivienne Sabler:** owner of the Velour. Controlled, magnetic, sells the evening but "not the morning."
  She knows more than she says, and knows Ines's name without being told.
* **Bruno:** the Velour's doorman. "I don't do personal."
* **Augie Prell:** a street photographer who supplements his income with blackmail. Hobb took his camera.
  He trades the photograph that links Tomas Reyes to the Velour's floor manager, **Lionel Gaunt**.
* **Sully:** knows everybody. Will trade information for a light.
* **Rosa:** a hotel maid on her break. Tells the rumour of Pim, who "came back empty" from the Sumps.
* **Hobb, Pike, and Danny and Mick Dunne:** a debt collector and his men.
* **Mrs. Kowal:** behind the door of 2B, listening to the Velour's band on the radio.
* **Mr. Haskell:** the Marquee's projectionist. His wife Margit had the night ward in Saint Agathe's east
  wing in 1931 and appears in the film he shows every night.
* **The hotel couple and the two dancers:** vignettes.

**Threads planted for the vertical slice:** Lionel Gaunt and the Velour; F. Dravo's cellar under Hu's
Laundry ("two donors Thursday"); Pim and Jo Fairweather's Lantern Society; Alderman Pruitt (the campaign
posters in the alley and the face in Augie's prints); Saint Agathe's east wing (the cut reel).

## Writing guidelines
* People talk in subtext. No one explains the plot.
* Humour and warmth are allowed and necessary.
* The Row's sex workers, dancers and maids have agency, opinions and their own business.
* Avoid noir clichés: no voice-over detective, no femme fatale as a plot device, no dead woman used as
  set dressing.
